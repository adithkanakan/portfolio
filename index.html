Time-Series Forecasting · Machine Learning
Hourly Energy Demand Forecasting System

End-to-end hourly electricity-demand forecasting and tariff-aware cost simulation for small commercial buildings, built on the I-BLEND smart-meter dataset (4 buildings, 2013–2017). LightGBM cut forecast error from 20.5% to 8.7% WAPE against a seasonal-naive baseline, and MAE from 2.15 to 0.91 kWh. It beat Random Forest and SARIMAX across all buildings in leakage-safe, rolling-origin backtests. Served through a 7-endpoint FastAPI service and a 6-page React dashboard, containerised with Docker Compose and CI, with 96 passing tests.

Healthcare · Computer Vision Published · IJSREM
HemoDetect: Blood Group Detection

A non-invasive deep learning system that predicts blood group from fingerprint images. A LeNet-5 CNN classifies 8 blood groups (A±, B±, AB±, O±) and reached 73.08% test accuracy after 30 epochs. Fingerprints were resized to 224×224, normalised and augmented, then trained on an 80/20 split with the Adam optimizer. The model is deployed as a Gradio web app for instant predictions from an uploaded fingerprint. Published in the International Journal of Scientific Research in Engineering and Management (IJSREM, Vol. 9, Issue 4, 2025).

Python TensorFlow Keras OpenCV CNN (LeNet-5) Gradio
